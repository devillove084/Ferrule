\begin{abstract}
Storage-backed inference needs bounded materialization and safe reuse, not
merely a small device cache. \system{} connects source-bound plans,
stage-local admission, exact completion identities and physical custody to
bounded weight residency. Its next-expert lookahead and safe blocking control
use the same positioned-read worker, host/device budgets and synchronous
forward. After nine source-qualified factory gates, a preregistered study of
Qwen3.5-35B-A3B completes five paired blocks, ten fresh server lifecycles and
120/120 HTTP requests. Worker-to-HTTP joins cover all 900 generated token IDs;
110 requests match exact eight-token oracles and ten are two-token smoke
checks. Every lifecycle records identical logical reader work, zero wasted
read bytes and a 16,777,984-byte logical host high-water below 64 MiB.
Device-wide samples reach 4,125 MiB against a 7 GiB policy, not a certified
physical peak. The exponentiated median paired log ratio gives 8.18\% lower
client E2E, 11.24\% lower TTFT and 10.91\% higher qualified serial-window
goodput with lookahead; all five blocks show less host demand waiting.
These are descriptive results from five blocks, not significance or
state-of-the-art claims. Shared NFS-client counters do not establish NAS
backend traffic, and host waits do not prove GPU overlap. Capacity frontiers,
integrated fault testing and post-drop physical release remain open;
physical-memory necessity and nonblocking execution are not claimed.
\end{abstract}
