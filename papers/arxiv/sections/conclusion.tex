\section{Limitations and Remaining Evidence Gates}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Bounded accounting and physical attribution.}
Configured budgets cover selected ledgers, not the complete process/machine
working set. Packed/prepared payload quota, metadata allowance and requested
worker stack are bounded on the implemented path, but OS/TLS/allocator
headroom and weak-reference-retained allocation backing are not physical RSS
bounds. Device/PSS/RSS samples can miss transients and lack complete
allocation attribution. Physical-memory insufficiency and storage necessity
are not claims of this scoped paper; they would additionally require
compact-residency, page-cache/swap, co-tenant and per-pool evidence. Shared
physical pools count once, and discrete CUDA records do not qualify
shared/unified-memory deployment.

\paragraph{Logical demand and same-cut quality.}
Historical TP1 \texttt{-02} records repeated application-level demand during
four HTTP successes in one DEBUG run. Its 36,240,064,128 reader bytes are not
physical NAS or backing-block traffic, and its plots are not current read-
pipeline statistics. Three visible-text/usage reference canaries passed;
Hello2 was HTTP/usage only, generated IDs were absent, and complete numerical
quality was not observed. Current \texttt{033519c} factory qualification
now passes all nine GPU gates, including both schedules' diagnostic,
ordinary-fingerprint and current-primary cold checks. Ordinary routes/states
remain unobserved, and stream denominators are not pooled. Historical
initialization and strict-wrapper failures remain failures in
Appendix~\ref{sec:historical-chronology}. Neither factory results nor common
CLI source/runtime provenance establish CLI/HTTP numerical equivalence.
Observer/allocator-getter cost is unmeasured, TP2 does not fill TP1 gates,
and argmax matches do not waive W8A16 strict failure.

\paragraph{Scheduling, integration, and progress.}
Both opt-in blocking and bounded lookahead are implemented, with the same
read worker and host/device budgets. Whole forwards remain synchronous;
blocking syscalls, consumer waits, locks and synchronization remain possible.
The read window is split from owner compute submission, not from every
consumer completion. Nsight-correlated kernel/copy evidence and a completed
same-cut paired study are still needed for GPU overlap or a scheduling
benefit. Host-span intersections and a worker thread establish neither.
The stopped five-block study supplies no estimates; corrective V3 remains
unqualified, not a retry or a power guarantee.
The nine gates provide one qualified factory/accounting point, not repeated
performance data or a capacity frontier. Logical host high-water is not RSS;
allocator high-water may be observed through a fence-polling getter, while
passive snapshots leave allocator state unobserved.

The lifecycle algorithms describe named boundaries, not formal verification
or universal registry integration. The prepared-KV, HostKV and specialized
expert paths have distinct owners. Passive observations neither heal poison
nor discharge custody. Host cancel/drain/join cannot prove GPU quiescence;
Unknown may permanently reduce available capacity. Final historical TP1
resource closure remains unknown despite process drain and idle observations.
No general reset/replay, device-reset recovery, bounded recovery time,
availability, durable ACID or arbitrary cross-rank atomicity is provided.
Mixed collectives retain their scoped host-default boundary. Other modalities
and shared/unified memory need independent implementation and quality gates.

\paragraph{Comparisons and reproducibility.}
The two resident TP2 crossblocks are descriptive tradeoffs, not a fair,
causal or statistically confirmed speedup. Arithmetic and total budgets
differ; startup observations have uncontrolled caches and unpaired context.
No state-of-the-art or first-storage-inference claim is made. Submission
requires portable source/binary/model/oracle identities, complete failure
records, deterministic tables/plots and final archival/visual checks. A
scoped logical/NFS-client report may retain unknown NAS backing traffic
without claiming storage necessity; it may not invent that missing evidence.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\system{} connects bounded storage-backed weight residency to source-bound
planning, covered admission, exact completion, authorized publication, and
custody through physical retirement. The concrete next-expert pipeline
provides a same-worker blocking control and one bounded lookahead read after
current upload and before compute submission. Its three-artifact plans,
alias-aware host quota and sticky Unknown handling make the scheduling
boundary and safe reuse conditions explicit without changing the synchronous
forward.

The frozen \texttt{033519c} factory/profile now passes all nine exact-once
GPU qualification gates for constructor/provider and both schedules' full40,
fresh, ordinary and cold-owner paths. Ordinary fingerprints match their own
schedule's diagnostic primary; streams and unobserved route/state fields keep
their distinct scope. This is a qualified numerical/provider and accounting
point, not CLI/HTTP equivalence, a capacity frontier or physical release.
Aligned client I/O, integrated fault/custody evidence and the five-pair RQ3
performance study remain open after the pre-Ready CLI failure. GPU overlap requires correlated activity,
not host submission spans; physical-memory necessity, general nonblocking
execution and performance leadership require stronger separate evidence.
