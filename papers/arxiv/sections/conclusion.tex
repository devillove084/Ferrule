\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Accounting is not the complete physical working set.}
The 64 MiB host limit bounds packed/prepared payload, metadata and requested
stack charges. OS stack rounding, TLS, allocator headroom, weak-reference
backing and file-cache residency are distinct. Device samples can miss
transients and do not provide exact process attribution. A qualified
configured-budget point does not establish a capacity frontier, compact-weight
residency impossibility or storage necessity. Other representations,
shared/unified memory and modalities require independent evidence.

\paragraph{One workload and five paired blocks.}
The current factory and HTTP checks apply to the frozen Mixed profile and
short prompts, not broad task quality, long-context behavior or all model
states. Ordinary factory routes/states and HTTP logits remain unobserved.
Requests within a server are not independent replicates. Fixed 3:2 order
balance and exact enumeration of 3,125 resamples do not guarantee interval
coverage or power; uncontrolled shared caches and instrumentation limit
external validity. The observed wait/latency association is not a causal
critical-path decomposition. Serial-window goodput excludes startup,
inter-request gaps and concurrency; it is not general serving throughput.
The unmatched TP2/vLLM records remain descriptive supplements.

\paragraph{Unobserved physical I/O, overlap and release.}
Logical reads can be cache-served, shared NFS deltas are not request-exclusive,
and NAS backing traffic is unknown. Startup/shutdown PID-I/O was not
collected. Both measured modes compile NVTX but run with profiling off;
the separate rejected capture supplies no physical-overlap result. Whole
forwards still synchronize on existing consumer proofs. Known owner-local
closed/retained state, zero logical charges, authenticated process drain and
idle samples do not certify post-drop physical release.

\paragraph{Faults and reproducibility boundaries.}
Component tests and lifecycle algorithms do not constitute formal
verification or integrated E5 fault acceptance. Unknown custody may retain
capacity indefinitely; no general reset/replay, availability, device-reset
recovery, durable ACID or arbitrary cross-rank atomicity is promised.
The portable archive supports offline receipt reconstruction, not independent
replay of omitted private authentication, live mapped-memory contents or
external model/native/executable payloads. Historical failures remain
failures, and source cuts are not pooled. No first-storage-inference or
state-of-the-art performance claim is made.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\system{} connects bounded storage-backed weight residency to source-bound
planning, covered admission, exact completion, authorized publication and
custody through retirement. Its one-next-expert read window preserves the
same worker, budgets and synchronous device-consumer proofs as the blocking
control. On a qualified current source cut, the five-block HTTP study
completes all 120 requests with joined token/terminal evidence, equal logical
reader work, zero wasted read bytes and bounded logical host charge.
Lookahead shows lower host demand waiting in every block and paired
summaries of 8.18\% lower E2E, 11.24\% lower TTFT and 10.91\% higher qualified
serial-window goodput. These are scoped measurements, not a performance
ranking or GPU-overlap proof. Capacity frontiers, integrated faults and
post-drop physical release remain open without weakening the reported
bounded-residency and measured-wait result.
