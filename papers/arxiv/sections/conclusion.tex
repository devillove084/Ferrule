\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Resource and deployment scope.}
The experiments bound selected logical accounts, not the complete physical
working set. The host limit includes packed and prepared payloads, metadata,
and requested worker stack; OS stack rounding, TLS, allocator overhead,
weak-reference backing, and file-cache residency require additional headroom.
Persistent-worker observations retain 10 MiB of logical metadata and stack
charge while the owner is idle and alive. Zero joins in those snapshots are
not evidence of a missing required live join or of a completed final-owner
join; the latter witness remains unobserved. Device samples can miss transient
peaks and lack exact process attribution. The three cache points vary entry
and byte limits together, so they neither isolate a replacement-policy effect
nor locate a physical capacity frontier. The complete compact checkpoint may
fit in host memory. Logical reads do not establish physical NAS traffic or
physical storage necessity.

\paragraph{Numerical and model scope.}
The evaluated Qwen3.5-35B-A3B-FP8 text profile decodes storage values to BF16
execution weights and uses explicit F32 accumulation and state boundaries.
Its numerical and HTTP checks do not establish full-model W8A16 quality,
stock dynamic-FP8 behavior, or same-precision equivalence to another engine.
DeepSeek-V4 end-to-end execution remains untested. Other precisions, adapters,
topologies, long contexts, shared-memory devices, and multimodal workloads
remain outside the evaluated profile.

\paragraph{Statistical and measurement scope.}
The earlier read-scheduling study has five paired blocks, not 50 independent
request repetitions. The transfer-policy pilot has two pairs per read schedule;
its 40 measured requests are nested observations, not 40 independent policy
replicates. Schedules, source cuts, and experiments are not pooled. Shared page
and NFS caches can carry state across fresh servers. The older descriptive
resampling ranges provide neither guaranteed coverage nor a significance or
power result; no intervals or significance claim are made for the newer pilot.
Older serial-window goodput excludes inter-request gaps, whereas the newer
phase-qualified goodput includes them between the first measured dispatch and
last measured terminal. Neither measures concurrent-server capacity or
startup-inclusive throughput. Client time to first nonempty content is not
first-device-token latency. Instrumentation overhead remains unmeasured.

The read--compute overlap study concerns two separate requests. Its scoped
checks pass, while wrapper warnings and an unknown dropped-event count leave
full-trace completeness unverified. The grouped-transfer profiles are also
partial selected-process observations: qualified pairs exist for both read
schedules under separately disclosed collection controls, but full-trace
acceptance is false and event loss remains unknown. Failed captures retain
their failed status. API and interval sums may overlap and cannot be added
into an exclusive explanation of elapsed time. Neither fewer synchronization
calls nor the retained-upload pin/free overhead identifies the exact cause of
unprofiled timing differences.

\paragraph{Failure and verification scope.}
The component experiment runs real CUDA operations but injects a software
failure or discards proof after valid fences. It does not exercise a driver
failure, lost hardware event, or full-model uncertain completion. The HTTP
experiment tests a client disconnect followed by a new request, not resumed
execution or recovery from a poisoned owner. Neither experiment establishes
the integrated five-class conservation invariant across a serving request.
Logical host/cache observations do not supply that missing ledger. Final
physical release remains unobserved; process drain, device idle, and local
lock release are not universal CUDA-release certificates. Unknown custody
can reduce availability indefinitely; the design supplies no general in-place
reset, durable crash recovery, or arbitrary cross-rank atomic commit.

\paragraph{Reproducibility scope.}
Public archives support offline reconstruction of the earlier paired results
and selected figures. The newer worker and transfer summaries instead retain
bounded derived evidence and hashes of local originals; their raw reports,
traces, binaries, and build inputs are not a complete portable public archive.
Receipt checks do not replay private authentication or recover omitted native
dependencies, model payloads, or live mapped-memory contents. Integrity hashes
are not external attestations. Historical rejected or incomplete studies
remain separate rather than becoming successful repetitions, and measured
source cuts do not automatically qualify a later cleaned implementation.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Bounded storage-backed weight residency requires a lifetime contract as well
as a cache policy. \system{} connects source-bound planning and stage-local
admission to exact completion, publication, and ownership through retirement.
The implemented expert path combines a persistent bounded read worker,
optional lookahead, and grouped synchronous uploads while preserving a
synchronous forward interface. Quality-checked serving and component
experiments support this architecture within one explicit numerical profile
and selected logical accounting domains.

The measurements also show why mechanism and performance claims must remain
separate. The earlier five-pair experiment isolates read scheduling; newer
whole-test and HTTP comparisons isolate transfer policy within each read
schedule. In the HTTP pilot, grouping improves observed latency and
phase-qualified goodput with lookahead but regresses time to first content
with blocking reads. A retained-upload alternative has a substantial negative
whole-test result. Partial profiles expose synchronization and host API costs
without proving a complete critical-path explanation. These findings support
conditional engineering choices, not universal superiority. Integrated fault
conservation, a physical capacity frontier, final physical release, and
broader model generality remain outside the demonstrated result.
