Entry-count reconciliation at observed invocation boundaries is
\begin{equation}
 N_j=N_{j-1}+I_j-E_j,\quad j=2,\ldots,30,\qquad
 N_0^{\mathrm{implied}}=N_1-I_1+E_1=0.
 \label{eq:entry-reconciliation}
\end{equation}
Initial zero is implied, not measured. All later boundary residuals are zero;
11,519 uploads minus 11,455 evictions equals 64 final observed entries.
This is neither byte conservation nor continuous occupancy. Compatible
resident, pending-upload and scratch gauges define the cache subset
\begin{equation}
 C_j^{\mathrm{cache}}=X_j+Q_j+S_j,\qquad
 C_{\mathrm{peak}}^{\mathrm{cache}}=
 \max_{u\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{owner}}}C_u^{\mathrm{cache}}
 \leq b_{\mathrm{cache}}.
 \label{eq:cache-charge}
\end{equation}
Unknown quarantine $Z_j$ is already charged, not another additive copy.
The reported 219,754,496-byte high-water covers cache update points under
the 320 MiB ceiling, not all physical-owner allocations. The four frozen
figures below retain these historical scopes and are not regenerated.
