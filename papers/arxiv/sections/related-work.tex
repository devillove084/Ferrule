\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Storage-backed inference.}
FlexGen~\cite{sheng2023flexgen} distributes tensors across GPU, CPU, and disk
and optimizes throughput-oriented schedules with compression and
attention-cache management. ZeRO-Inference~\cite{deepspeed2022zeroinference}
describes CPU or NVMe weight hosting, layer streaming, prefetch, and release.
PowerInfer-2~\cite{xue2024powerinfer2} targets smartphone inference beyond
resident capacity using neuron clusters, segmented caching, and an
I/O/computation pipeline. These systems establish weight streaming and
transfer/computation scheduling as prior work. \system{} focuses on the
contract that links each materialization stage to its resource claims,
completion identity, and eventual reuse.

\paragraph{Resident offload and state management.}
PowerInfer~\cite{song2023powerinfer} exploits activation locality by executing
hot neurons on a GPU and cold neurons on a CPU. This resident-domain hybrid
differs from PowerInfer-2's storage pipeline. vLLM's
PagedAttention~\cite{kwon2023pagedattention} addresses serving KV memory through
paged allocation; it is relevant to state lifetime but is not the same
operation as request-time weight materialization. A fair comparison must
specify the representation, physical memory pools, arithmetic, and quality
criterion. The separate resident vLLM observations in
Appendix~\ref{sec:resident-supplement} do not match those conditions and are
not used to estimate a comparative speedup.

\paragraph{Scheduling and lifetime.}
An asynchronous interface, an independent progress worker, and hardware
overlap are distinct properties. The comparison in this paper holds worker,
representation, and resource limits fixed, then changes when one next-expert
read is submitted. Client timing measures the resulting behavior; correlated
file-call and kernel intervals separately measure observed overlap. Neither
the presence of a worker nor an intersection of broad host ranges is used as
a substitute for device activity.

\system{} contributes a profile-scoped account of source binding, stage-local
admission, publication, and retirement, together with measurements that keep
logical and physical observations separate. We have not exhaustively audited
the custody or failure semantics of the systems above and do not claim that
they lack equivalent mechanisms. The empirical contribution is the controlled
comparison and its explicit resource and completion scope, not a first-system
or state-of-the-art claim.
